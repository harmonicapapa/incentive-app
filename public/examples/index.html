How it's calculated

  • Every completed task adds its value. Missed tasks add £0 and never remove earlier earnings.
  • The attendance bonus depends only on college days missed: every two missed days drops one level. Seven or more missed means no bonus.
  • Weekly tasks can be done once per Monday–Sunday week, up to 4 times a month.
  • The examples assume a 31-day month, 16 college days and no excused days.
